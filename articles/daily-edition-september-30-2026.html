The Fine Print Picks the Car

An insurance quote picked an Ioniq 5, British wiring rules split Hoymiles' solar from its battery, and US policy shrank GM's Bolt run to a fraction.

nexusEVnews  ·  Sep 30, 2026

A $5,500 insurance quote did more to put one Ontario family in an electric car than any range figure. In The Straight Pipes' ownership update, Jacob says a five-year-old Lexus SUV and a Genesis GV60 both drew quotes around that number, while a used Hyundai Ioniq 5 came in near $3,000 a year. The EV won on paperwork. Across today's five stories, the numbers deciding outcomes sit in insurance quotes, warranty extensions, wiring regulations and price lists, not on spec sheets.

Warranty is the second document. Jacob says he will not own the car out of warranty, and his ICCU failure shows what is at stake. Hyundai has extended ICCU coverage on 2022 to 2024 Ioniq 5s in Canada to 15 years or 290,000 km, according to Guide Auto Web, which moves the best-known risk on that car from the owner to the manufacturer. In Britain the deciding document is a statutory instrument. Plug-in solar became legal to self-install on 27 August, but only for panel kits up to 800 W AC with no battery, per UK solar guides summarising SI 2026/848. That is why Hoymiles' plug-in microinverters can be sold as plug-and-play while its stackable battery still needs an installer.

In the US, the paperwork has been torn up, and Autoline Daily shows what follows. The federal EV tax credit ended a year ago today, on September 30, 2025, and Autoline reports the administration has now finalized fuel economy rules that lower the 2031 target to 34.9 mpg. GM, per a UAW official cited by the show, is on pace for about 35,000 Bolts, against production volume that was once tracking near 150,000. The commercial clean vehicle credit of up to $40,000 ended on the same date, so US fleets now weigh the Tesla Semi without that federal help. Dan Priestley's focus, on Munro Live, is taking cost out of the build through shared hardware: a thermal system shared with Cybercab, a stator from the Cybertruck line, one battery pack used in twos and threes.

Then there is the price list itself. Audi's SQ6 e-tron is now about $21,400 cheaper in Australia, with more standard equipment, and Ben Alexxander ties the cut to buyers drifting toward BYD, XPeng and Zeekr. Volkswagen Group, Audi's parent, is at the same time deepening its partnership with Chinese battery maker Gotion to make LFP cells in Europe. Competing against Chinese brands on price and partnering with a Chinese supplier on cells are now happening inside the same company.

What to watch: whether British rules extend plug-in status to batteries, which the Hoymiles video hopes happens in 2027; Audi's final range figure for the 2027 SQ6; how many fleets order Semis with no federal help; and whether the redesigned ICCU that Hyundai's German arm says went into Ioniq 5s built after April 2024 holds up. For anyone shopping a used Ioniq 5 today, the build date may matter more than the trim.

Used EVs

Nine Months With a Used Ioniq 5: Insurance Chose It, the ICCU Tested It

The Straight Pipes

Jacob from The Straight Pipes bought his wife's 2023 Hyundai Ioniq 5 used, in Ultimate trim, for about $40,000 Canadian with roughly 30,000 km on it. Nine months later it shows 48,000. Insurance made the call, he says: quotes near $5,500 a year for a newer used Lexus SUV and a Genesis GV60, against about $3,000 for the Hyundai. His wife also wanted Ontario's green plates, which allow solo driving in HOV lanes. His running numbers are the useful part. He reports about 480 km of summer range in the app, 80 to 100 km less in winter, and roughly 20 percent of the battery used on a daily commute. Installing a level 2 charger, which he says Autel supplied, cost about $2,000, and he counts around $600 for the unit in his savings math. Off-peak charging runs $40 to $50 a month against the $120 a week his wife used to spend on fuel. The low point was an ICCU failure: a loud pop, reduced power, a same-day tow and a quick dealer repair. He believes the only remedy is another replacement. Hyundai's German arm says Ioniq 5s built after April 2024 carry a redesigned unit, per electrive, and Hyundai Auto Canada has extended ICCU coverage on 2022 to 2024 cars to 15 years or 290,000 km, according to Guide Auto Web.

Solar

Hoymiles Brings Plug-In Microinverters and a Stackable Battery to the UK

UpsideDownFork

UpsideDownFork visited Hoymiles' stand at Solar and Storage Live, at the company's invitation, and the timing matters. Plug-in solar became legal to self-install in Great Britain on 27 August under SI 2026/848, capped at 800 W AC, one device per household circuit and no battery, according to UK solar guides that summarise the rules. Hoymiles' plug-in kit is built for that limit. The video shows an 800 W microinverter with a single input that accepts up to 100 volts, so two large panels can run in series, and a two-input 800 W version for panels facing different directions. The batteries are the part still waiting. The host says Hoymiles' stackable system is designed for plug-in use and sold that way on the continent, but in the UK it must be hardwired by a qualified installer, and he hopes that changes in 2027. The details he lists: 4 kWh LFP modules that stack, four solar inputs rated at 1,000 W each, a backup port for essentials during a power cut, a wired RS485 port, MQTT support for Home Assistant, an IP66 rating, heating pads for operation down to minus 20 degrees, 8,000 rated cycles, and a display unit he says will weigh 42 kg once its cells are fitted. No UK prices are given.

Deep Dive

Tesla's Semi Reuses Cybercab and Cybertruck Hardware to Cut Build Cost

Munro Live

Munro Live sat down with Tesla Semi program director Dan Priestley, and his theme was reuse. Priestley says the pilot fleet, running since the end of 2022, has covered about 17.5 million miles, and those lessons fed a redesign aimed at lower build cost and higher reliability. The truck now uses Tesla's in-house 4680 cells instead of purchased 2170 cells. Its thermal system is what Tesla calls the indirect thermal system, which he says is built in Texas, shared with Cybercab and keeps all refrigerant inside one assembly. It replaces a pair of Model 3 and Model Y units that ran at 400 volts inside a 1,000-volt truck. The motor stator comes from the Cybertruck line with modifications, while the rotor is unique to Semi because of its duty cycle. The 500-mile long-range truck uses three identical packs and the standard-range truck two, which he says keeps one line and one parts supply. The plant is designed for 50,000 trucks a year, about 1,000 a week. A sleeper cab is not coming yet, Priestley says, because no over-the-road charging network exists to support one, so Tesla is aiming at day cab local and regional work first, with hardware designed to be ready for autonomy. US fleets now weigh that pitch without the federal commercial clean vehicle credit of up to $40,000, which ended September 30, 2025.

News

Audi Cuts the SQ6 e-tron's Australian Price by About $21,400

Ben Alexxander

Audi Australia has reset the SQ6 e-tron's list price rather than discounting stock. Ben Alexxander reports the model year 2027 SUV at $129,900 before on-road costs, down from $151,300, a cut of about $21,400, with the Sportback falling by the same amount to $134,900. Production starts in October and deliveries are expected in the first half of 2027, per the video. Audi is adding equipment rather than removing it. A black exterior styling package becomes standard, joining adaptive air suspension, 21-inch wheels, an augmented reality head-up display, massaging front seats and a 16-speaker Bang and Olufsen system. The powertrain carries over: two motors making 360 kW, or 380 kW with launch control, and 0 to 100 km/h in 4.3 seconds. The battery is 100 kWh gross and 94.9 kWh usable, on an 800-volt system that charges at up to 270 kW, with 10 to 80 percent quoted at 21 minutes. The 585 km WLTP figure belongs to the current car, and the host says Audi has not published a 2027 number. The regular Q6 e-tron also falls to $89,900, nearly $10,000 lower, which he reads as a family-wide repositioning against BYD, XPeng and Zeekr. By his count, Chinese brands now account for close to 40 percent of Australian new car sales.

News

VW Deepens Its Gotion Ties as GM's Bolt Run Falls Far Short

Autoline Network

Autoline Daily's episode 4387 covers several threads. Volkswagen is sharing European battery production with Gotion, the Chinese cell maker in which it took a stake of about 24 percent in 2020, per the show. VW will sell 49 percent of its Spanish battery operations to Gotion for just over one billion euros, while VW's PowerCo takes 49 percent of Gotion's plants in Slovakia and Morocco for 470 million euros. Those sites will shift toward LFP cells and cathode material, and VW expects LFP's European share to climb from about 10 percent today to 40 to 60 percent by 2030. In China, GAC wants to buy FAW's half of FAW Toyota, and Changan will share admin, R&D and manufacturing across Avatr and Deepal. In the US, S&P Global Mobility forecasts 16.1 million sales this year and 16.4 million by the end of the decade, still below the pre-pandemic 17 million, with hybrids expected to reach nearly a third of North American assembly. GM, according to a UAW official cited by the show, is on pace for about 35,000 Bolts, against production volume once tracking toward 150,000, before the model ends early next year. The Department of Transportation claims its new rules save GM $20 billion in technology costs.

Bottom line: The most useful document in today's lineup is an insurance quote, and any EV shopper should get three before booking a test drive. Jacob's Ioniq 5 math works because the premium was $2,500 lower and the ICCU risk now sits with Hyundai for 15 years, so anyone buying an early car should confirm that coverage against the VIN. Audi's cut is real value, but $130,000 still buys a badge more than a better car. Hoymiles' battery is worth waiting on until British rules catch up. And GM's Bolt shows how quickly policy can shrink a production plan.
